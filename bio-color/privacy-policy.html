Bio-Color · Relax & Puzzle

Privacy Policy

How the Android game handles your information.

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Developer: iogusnohin
App: Bio-Color: Relax & Puzzle
Android package: jp.aasagyou.colorculture

At a glance: Bio-Color is an offline relaxation game. It does not require an account, display ads, use an analytics or tracking SDK, or send your gameplay data to the developer. Game records are stored on your device and can be exported only when you choose to save them.

1. Information we collect

Bio-Color does not ask you to register or provide your name, address, phone number, email address, or other directly identifying information to play. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to a developer-operated server.

The current app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

2. Information stored on your device

To provide the game and keep your play history, the app stores data locally using Android app storage, including WebView local storage and IndexedDB. This data may include:

  • Game actions, selected energy levels, and the state of the colored cells.
  • Scores, color coverage, game duration, and completed-round history.
  • Game snapshots and technical diagnostic records used to check performance or troubleshoot the app.

These records remain on your device. They are not automatically uploaded or sent to the developer. The app may also maintain temporary data required for normal operation.

3. Internet access, advertising, and third-party services

The current Android version works offline and does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Its game content is included in the application package and displayed using Android System WebView.

The app currently does not integrate AdMob, an advertising SDK, an analytics SDK, an account service, or a third-party tracking service. It does not send gameplay data to an external service.

If advertising, analytics, online services, or additional third-party SDKs are introduced in a future version, this policy will be reviewed and updated to describe the applicable data practices.

4. Exporting game logs

The app provides an optional feature to export game records in JSON format. When you choose to export, Android asks you to select a save location. The resulting file may contain the game events, scores, and snapshots described above.

Exported files are saved to the location you choose. They are not sent to the developer by the export feature. If you later share or upload an exported file, the receiving service or person may be able to access its contents.

5. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to normal Android device behavior. You can remove it through Android Settings > Apps > Bio-Color > Storage > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

JSON files that you explicitly exported outside the app's private storage are not necessarily deleted when you uninstall the app. You can delete those files using your device's file manager.

6. Security and children's privacy

We minimize data handling by keeping the game offline and storing its records locally. Device-level protection depends on the Android system and your device settings. Please take care when sharing exported files.

The app itself does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no in-app accounts or features for submitting personal information to the developer.

7. Support communications

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email, your email address and message will be used to respond to your inquiry and investigate the issue. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information or sensitive game records. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for support and applicable obligations.

8. This website and external links

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which is separate from the offline Android app. When you view this page or follow an external link in your browser, the website provider may process ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and browser details, under its own privacy practices. No advertising or analytics scripts have been added to this policy page by the developer.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features, data practices, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.
